Why Attribute table exists ? 

    External System specific fields which came later into the application and need to manage it within less time can be stored in attr. 
    Sudden change and for a specific period of time say discount on some customers.

Why does Product Category Attr Table exists ? What can we store in it ?

    We can unlimited attributes dynamically without changing the schema.
    We can add attributes with custom names coming from external systems like shopify.

What is Product Catalog ?

    It is type of marketing collateral (media used to inform, educate, and guide prospects and customers) that list essential details about the product so that buyers make a purchase decision.
    These details include product features, descriptions, dimensions, price, weight, availability, color, customer reviews, and more.



What is Product Category ?

    A product category is a grouping of related products that share a common purpose, audience, or attribute. Think of it as the organizational backbone of your eCommerce store, the system that tells both shoppers and search engines what you sell and how to find it.
