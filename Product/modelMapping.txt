

Admin API  vs StoreFront API

- Admin side used to manage store data and configurations.
(orders , products , customers , shipping , inventory , routing , proximity)
- StoreFront is used for shopping experience and has nothing to do with complex logics.
(displaying powerful pdp attracting customers to interact with website , displaying carts , products and collections , variants available)
